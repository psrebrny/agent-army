# Page copy — landing and pricing pages, as DRAFT

Read this once the user has accepted the positioning statement and the offer. `SKILL.md` rules still
apply; above all, every claim traces to an `F-n` or an `A-n` with a test (rule 3), and nothing is
invented (rule 2).

1. **Landing page**, in this order: headline (the value for the best-fit customer, in their words) ·
   sub-headline (who it is for and the alternative it replaces) · three proof points, each traced ·
   how it works in three steps · answers to the top objections from the objection map · call to action
   that matches the offer (pre-order, book a call, start a trial). Social proof only from real,
   consented quotes or counts in the register; otherwise a labelled empty slot.
2. **Pricing page**: the package(s) and price from `business-case.md` Pricing, what is included and
   excluded, the billing period, any risk reversal under `SKILL.md` rule 5, and the
   answers to price objections. VAT shown as the business case states it; open VAT or consumer-rights
   questions go to `/legal-review`.
3. **Claims trace**: one row per claim on either page, `claim | ID | evidence level`. Cut any claim
   without an ID before showing the copy. Show one page at a time for review.
4. **Hand-off**: a `fix` work item per page with the final copy, its claims trace and acceptance
   (`a visitor sees …`, `the call to action leads to …`). Messages worth testing go to `/go-to-market`
   as bets (rule 7).

Write both pages under `Landing page copy (DRAFT)` and `Pricing page copy (DRAFT)` in
`positioning-offer.md`, and the trace under `Claims trace`.
