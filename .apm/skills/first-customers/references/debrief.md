# Debrief — after the user sent a card and pasted the results

Read this when the user says a card was sent and brings what came back (replies, comments, sign-ups,
payments). `SKILL.md` rules 1–10 still apply; this adds how to read a round.

1. **Count from what was pasted.** Quote the counts per card and channel. Personal data in the paste
   (names, handles, emails, screenshots with profiles) is replaced by tracker IDs before anything is
   written; say you did.
2. **Compare with the card's `P-n`**: fill its result column with the counts and the date, against the
   threshold and kill line written before sending. Never move the threshold. Below the volume the
   prediction named, write "not enough signal" and draw no comparison between channels or messages.
3. **Record facts at their level**, each as the next free `F-n` with source (card, channel, tracker ID)
   and date, proposed to the brief as Change proposals:
   - paid, pre-ordered, signed = `committed behavior` — the evidence `/product` needs for stage 13;
   - replied, booked a call, signed up, used a trial = `behavior`;
   - "I'd buy", "sounds great", likes = `stated intent` (compliments are not evidence).
4. **Update the tracker** rows (stage, last contact, next follow-up, evidence level) by ID only.
5. **Contradictions**: a result that contradicts a key `A` (price, segment, problem) is named with its
   row and handed to `/validate-product` (to test it) or `/positioning-offer` (to change the offer); you
   do not re-plan the product here.
6. **Next step and kill criterion**: one step of at most one week (usually the next card or a
   follow-up card) and a kill criterion per channel or segment ("stop group B after one more post with
   no reply").

Append to `first-customers.md` under Debriefs, one row per card:
`date | card | P-n | result vs threshold | facts F-n | not enough signal? | next step | kill criterion`.
